Delete your Bloomora account

Bloomora: Garden Match 3, by Md. Sahadat Hosen

You only have a Bloomora account if you chose Save with Google in the Android app. Playing without signing in creates no account.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Bloomora and tap the gear (Settings).
  2. Tap Cloud Save: On.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then Delete.

Or ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, email dev.sahadat@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Bloomora account". We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your cloud save (levels, stars, coins, boosters and other game progress).
  • Your friends entry (first name, level and stars shown to friends you invited or who invited you).
  • Your invite code and the record of the code you joined with.
  • Your Bloomora sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).

What is kept

If friends joined Bloomora with your invite code, their own invite record keeps your anonymous user ID (no name or email), so their rewards keep working. Nothing else is kept on our side. The progress saved on your phone stays there until you use Reset Progress in Settings or uninstall the game. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which handles its own data under Google's privacy policy.

See also the Bloomora privacy policy.
